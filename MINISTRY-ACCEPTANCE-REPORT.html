The instrument, clause by clause الصك، بنداً بنداً

An account of the National Health and Medical Readiness platform: where every clause of the Ministry's own instruments is discharged, what the build decided on its own authority, and what remains for the Ministry to decide.

Version 1.0 29 August 2026 Acceptance record Describes build 6d81562

1The answer

الخلاصة

Can an organizer take an event from creation to a recorded determination, and can the Ministry record every outcome the regulation gives it, without a screen where the next step does not exist?
Yes — in Arabic and in English, end to end. نعم — بالعربية والإنكليزية، من البداية إلى النهاية.

An organizer creates an event, answers the nine assessment domains, watches the level derive, attaches what that level requires, declares and certifies, and files. A Ministry reference number is issued. A reviewer reads every answer, opens every document, and records one of the three outcomes. The organizer reads that determination and prints the certificate they hand to the authorising authority.

Thirty-seven clauses were taken from the two instruments and their annexes and checked against the software as it stands. Section 2 lists every one; these are the totals.

Partly discharged does not mean failed. It means the clause has a screen and one limb of it does not. Both cases are the same shape: a power the instrument gives the Ministry rather than an obligation it places on anyone, and in both the missing limb is a publishing surface nobody has yet specified.

§9 lists nine portal capabilities. Eight are built. The ninth — national surveillance, audit and quality-improvement reporting — has no screen: the Ministry can read its own register and its own activity, and there is nothing that produces a national report from them. §14 says the Ministry may publish aggregated national findings that identify no patient. Nothing prevents it and nothing yet performs it.

Neither blocks a single filing, a single review or a single determination. Both become buildable the moment the Ministry says what a national report should contain.

The two deferred items are capability the product specification asks for and the regulation does not: the assistance layer, and fee and advertising capability. Both are recorded in section 6 with the reason and the condition that would change them. They are counted separately from the thirty-seven because neither is a clause of the Ministry's instruments.

The platform records health and medical preparedness. It does not authorize events. Authorization remains with the legally competent authority, and every determination this platform issues says so on its own face.

3What the build decided on its own authority

ما بتّت فيه المنصة من تلقاء نفسها

Where the instruments were silent, ambiguous, or disagreed with each other, the build had to do something. Each of these is a decision somebody made in order to ship, and each is reversible. They are listed so the Ministry can ratify or overturn them rather than discover them.

3.1

The minimum conditions are the union of both issues شروط الحد الأدنى هي اتحاد الإصدارين

Annex A Part D carries nine conditions in the English issue and nine in the Arabic, and they are not the same nine. The build derives against the union — ten. Some events therefore receive a level that neither issue alone would give them.

3.2

Where the two issues differ, the English governs عند اختلاف الإصدارين، يحكم النص الإنكليزي

Eight divergences are recorded. The largest: the Arabic issue of the compliance form carries no insurance declaration at all. The build declares it, follows the English, and marks the Arabic wording as a translation pending a Ministry decision. Every divergence is shown on the screen where it bites, not hidden in a file.

3.3

Evidence of insurance is a file, not an answer إثبات التأمين ملف لا إجابة

The form asked whether evidence was attached, and the word “yes” satisfied it. It is now an attachment: the declaration cannot be made without a document behind it, and the Ministry opens that document during review. In force from 28 August 2026.

3.4

A new requirement applies forward, and never blocks in silence المتطلب الجديد يسري من تاريخه ولا يحجب بصمت

A submission already filed and determined stands. One filed and not yet determined is asked for the new document through the ordinary revision route — the reviewer records a revision outcome and the organizer is told what is missing. No filed submission becomes silently unfileable. The effective date is a value the Ministry sets, not the date the software shipped.

3.5

A determination is recorded once; changing it is a second act تُسجَّل النتيجة مرة واحدة؛ وتغييرها فعل ثانٍ

The instruments say what the three outcomes are, not what happens when one changes. The build records a determination once and then shows it. A revision is a separate, deliberate act that requires a written reason, is sent to the organizer in the reviewer's own words, and never erases the determination it replaces — both stay on the record, marked.

3.6

An unset value means nothing is in force القيمة غير المحددة تعني أن لا شيء سارياً

Where the Ministry has not published a threshold, a schedule or a cadence, screens say that nothing is in force under that heading and that operators are told when it is. No default is invented, and no requirement is implied. For three of the six facility categories this is the live answer today, and the public check names the missing value rather than returning a bare “no”.

3.7

The Order of Physicians lane is off, and the Ministry records its items meanwhile مسار نقابة الأطباء مطفأ، وتسجّل الوزارة بنوده في هذه الأثناء

The lane is configurable, non-determinative, and off by default; nothing from it reaches the facility side. While it is off, the attestation items assigned to the Order are recordable by the Ministry — otherwise the satisfied outcome would be permanently unreachable at Level 3. Assignment is unchanged: the row still names the Order.

3.8

Demonstration records exist in production; the means of making them does not السجلات التوضيحية موجودة في الإنتاج؛ أما أداة إنشائها فلا

The Ministry can walk the platform on real screens with real records that are marked as demonstrations. They are excluded from the national register, from every aggregate and from every reviewer queue, in both directions. What is disabled in a deployed environment is the tool that creates them, not the records themselves.

3.9

No annex is ever named to a user لا يُسمّى أي ملحق للمستخدم

A person is asked for the risk assessment, the compliance and submission form, the event health and medical plan — never for a letter. The instruments number their parts; the people who have to comply with them do not. This extends to search: a person searching by annex letter finds nothing, because matching a letter nobody is shown would teach a vocabulary the platform deliberately avoids.

4Open decisions, and what silence costs

قرارات معلّقة، وثمن السكوت عنها

None of these is a defect. Each needs a name against it, and each has a consequence if it is left. They are ordered by what silence costs, heaviest first.

4.1

Where the register physically lives أين يستقر السجل فعلياً

The store is a local file suited to review. Production storage, and the country it sits in, wait on a data-residency answer.

If nothing is decided

The platform cannot carry real filings. Nothing else on this list matters until this one is answered.

4.2

How people receive what the platform sends كيف يتلقى الناس ما ترسله المنصة

Nomination links, notifications and account activation links all assume a mail path that has not been chosen.

If nothing is decided

Every link is handed over by a person. An organizer nominating an ambulance service must carry the link to them by hand, and a Ministry administrator must do the same for every account they create. It works, and it does not scale past a small number of events.

4.3

The eight divergences between the two issues الاختلافات الثمانية بين الإصدارين

Including the Arabic compliance form carrying no insurance declaration, and a declaration item whose Arabic imposes a stronger obligation than its English.

If nothing is decided

The English governs by default on all eight. An Arabic-speaking organizer is held to at least one requirement their own issue of the form does not state — which is defensible in a review and not defensible in a dispute.

4.4

Annex A Part D — nine conditions in each issue, a different nine تسعة شروط في كل إصدار، وليست الشروط نفسها

The build carries the union of ten. That reconciliation was made in order to ship and has not been ratified.

If nothing is decided

Some events are assigned a minimum level that neither issue alone assigns them. The level is the number every requirement in the instrument hangs from, so this is the open decision with the widest reach.

4.5

How people prove who they are كيف يثبت الناس هويتهم

Sign-in and password storage exist. Whether the Ministry wants single sign-on, email verification, or a particular complexity rule does not.

If nothing is decided

Accounts are issued one at a time by an administrator, and a person who loses access needs the Ministry to issue a new link. No administrator ever sets or sees a password — that part is settled and will not change.

4.6

The device-check cadence and the lapse window دورية فحص الأجهزة ومهلة الانقضاء

Provisional figures sit in the configuration; the policy names none.

If nothing is decided

No cadence is in force. Facilities register their defibrillators and nothing ever falls due — the screens say so plainly rather than inventing a schedule, but a registry that never expires is a registry that ages.

4.7

How a covered facility is cited كيف يُشار إلى المنشأة المشمولة

No document defines a reference scheme for facilities. They carry an internal record identifier only.

If nothing is decided

A facility cannot be cited in correspondence except by name and address. Events have a Ministry reference number and facilities do not, which will be noticed the first time one is the subject of a letter.

4.8

What the platform owner may see ما يجوز لمالك المنصة الاطلاع عليه

One document says counts only; another says full visibility. The narrower reading is built, and it is reversible.

If nothing is decided

Counts only. That seat sees how many organizations, events and determinations exist and cannot see a single name. If the Ministry wants more, it is a privacy decision to take deliberately rather than a setting to change quietly.

4.9

Whether the platform ever charges هل تتقاضى المنصة رسماً يوماً ما

The capability exists behind switches and is off. Every screen that mentions cost says Fee: None.

If nothing is decided

Nothing commercial ever renders. This is the least urgent item on the list and the easiest to leave.

4.10

Who staffs the powers the matrix assigns من يشغل الصلاحيات التي تسندها المصفوفة

The platform reports, on the administrator's own screen, any power the permission matrix assigns that no active account holds. Three are unheld today: the Order of Physicians verification, and the two platform-owner surfaces.

If nothing is decided

Those powers stay advertised and unusable. A power held by nobody reachable is worse than a power nobody has, because the screen names an owner who does not exist — which is why the platform now says so out loud instead of leaving it to be discovered.

Every journey the Ministry will care about was completed, not merely visited — the party finished what the regulation asks and the record on the other side changed. The journey from creating an event to printing a determination completes in Arabic as well as English, from the first request rather than after a language toggle.

Three checks are worth naming because they are the ones that keep the rest honest. The level is never chosen anywhere, and a test proves no operation exists that could set one. An incident report is refused if a person's name appears in the narrative. And the public register discloses four facts and never a fifth, behind a second factor so it cannot be read by counting upwards.

The most useful thing found during this work was a class of defect rather than any single one: checks that passed without checking. A rule with a passing test and nothing calling it. A sign-in helper that reported success without signing in. A certificate that would have printed a blank page, invisible on screen because only paper would have shown it. Each is now guarded, and each guard was proved by breaking the thing it watches.

6Deliberately not built

ما لم يُبنَ عمداً

An absent feature with no record looks exactly like one somebody forgot. Both of these were considered, decided against for the reason given, and carry the condition under which they would be built.

6.1

The assistance layer طبقة المساعدة

The product specification describes assistants that would help an organizer through the assessment and help a reviewer read a submission. The regulation does not require them, and nothing in a Ministry review depends on one — every determination in this platform is reached by a person reading a record.

Building them would mean deciding what an assistant may see: an organizer's answers, a reviewer's notes, an incident narrative. That is a data-protection question, not a feature question, and it deserves its own conversation rather than a default set by whoever implements it first.

6.2

Fee, vendor and advertising capability إمكانات الرسوم والموردين والإعلانات

The capability exists because the product specification requires it to. It ships switched off, and nothing commercial renders anywhere in the Lebanon deployment. Capability is not content.

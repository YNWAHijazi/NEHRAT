# Revised Annex B — 29 September 2026

Status: local implementation, not published. The owner resolved the plan-completion rule on 29 September 2026.

## Source and confirmed decision

The owner supplied `ANNEX B Revised.docx` and `الملحق ب.docx`. Copies are retained in `source-documents/2026-09-29/annex-b-en.docx` and `annex-b-ar.docx`.

Both languages contain the same 19 requirements. Both say that the Level 3 Event Health and Medical Plan is required and prepared and approved by the Event Medical Director. The owner assigns electronic plan entry to either the confirmed Medical Director or EMS agency, never the organizer.

**Confirmed owner instruction (29 September 2026):** either the confirmed EMS agency or the confirmed Event Medical Director may complete the shared medical plan. Their completion is sufficient; there is no separate Director approval. This explicitly overrides the source documents’ Director-only preparation/approval clause. The required Level 3 Director nomination and the Ministry’s own review remain unchanged.

## Shared plan and organizer view

- Organizers have read-only medical-plan access at every level. Server actions reject organizer edits and uploads.
- Confirmed, event-linked Medical Directors and EMS agencies can prepare the same plan at Levels 2 and 3. Unconfirmed/removed parties, other events and the opposite demonstration scope cannot access it.
- Saved changes update clean medical-team forms and organizer views automatically. Unsaved edits are preserved and stale writes are rejected by the existing version check.
- Both organizer team tabs show the single shared plan, status, last editor and version. The Director tab also shows governance and the deployment map; each EMS tab entry shows its shared operational details, signed declaration and shared documents. Unsigned declaration drafts remain private.
- A stored filename without attachment bytes does not complete a plan.
- Level 2 plan requests use the existing Ministry additional-measures action. An active request changes the document to required in requirements, filing and Ministry views. Clearing it restores the recommendation.
- No database schema migration is required by these changes.

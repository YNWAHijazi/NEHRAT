Source: partner-supplied `PAD Annexes GIS Ready.docx`, SHA-256 `b4e1c3b0d92070a3418ddc41f2f9747be09b8aa8c96010b7809f0af64e7908e4`. Extracted text is in `sources/pad-annexes-gis-ready-20260926.txt`. The document was rendered and inspected; no tracked changes or comments were present.

Scope is the third service, Facilities: Annexes A–D. The user left scope to our judgment after clarifying this is the facilities module. Annex E describes EMS agency readiness and a separate reporting dataset; this release does not add an EMS agency reporting portal.

## Implemented

- Annex A: registration remains available for all six categories. AED obligations remain category-specific. Sports/aquatic facilities and the named transport/mall types require AEDs. Public venues use the published capacity threshold (strictly above). Education, remote facilities, prior-arrest facilities and other designations retain Ministry review where the applicable schedule/decision is not established. Authorized Ministry reviewers can record a facility-specific requirement with a reason; fixed requirements cannot be waived. Decisions are retained in history.
- Annex B: facility profile, one responsible contact, EMS entrance and number, operating details and a confirmed facility map pin. AED details populate the response plan. Six readiness checks and a drill within the previous 12 months are required. Required missing AEDs, unavailable/non-operational registered devices, or a missing pin block confirmation. Changes to facility arrangements, AED records or the applicable Ministry decision require a fresh confirmation. Previous confirmations and snapshots remain stored.
- Annex C: one record per AED with identifier, exact location, access, public availability, operating status and pediatric capability. Each inherits the facility location unless a separate confirmed map pin is chosen. Registration, relocation, replacement, operational/access changes and Ministry-requested updates are supported and logged. Existing optional maintenance dates remain available under an expandable section.
- Annex D: facility-linked date/time/location, immediate response, EMS attendance/transport, two post-incident questions and corrective action text. Submission records the authorized account and timestamp automatically; no repeated signature/contact form. Patient-name detection runs in the browser and on the server. Submitted reports are read-only, with organizer and Ministry history views.
- Ministry: facility/AED map, protected GeoJSON download, incident reports and AED decision history. Account permissions and demo/real boundaries apply before access. Maps are not a public directory.

Map pins are confirmed by the facility representative, not independently surveyed or verified by the Ministry. Coordinates are generated by the map; no manual coordinate entry. Older records retain their data and need a confirmed facility pin added before registering a new AED or confirming the plan. No coordinates are invented for old records.

OpenStreetMap tiles are loaded in the browser with attribution, standard caching and no bulk download. GPS is optional and only requested when the user presses Use my location. Automated journey tests mock tile images; real tile availability is checked separately.
